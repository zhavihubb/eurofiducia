Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before opening your EuroFiducia account.

EuroFiducia operates in alignment with the EU MiFID II framework and works within French ACPR/AMF and Italian CONSOB regulatory expectations. Client onboarding includes full KYC/AML verification, client funds are held in segregated accounts at tier-1 European banks, and eligible assets are covered by European investor protection schemes.

You can open an account with no minimum balance, and start investing from €100. Fractional investing lets you buy portions of expensive instruments — for example, a slice of the CAC 40 or S&P 500 index — so you never need thousands to begin.

Client money is held in segregated accounts, completely separate from company operating funds. Data is protected with 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption and two-factor authentication. In the unlikely event of platform failure, eligible assets remain yours and are covered by European investor protection schemes up to €100,000 per investor.

Our fee structure is fully transparent: no account opening fees, no inactivity fees, and no hidden charges. You pay a clear custody fee on assets, transaction fees on trades, and loan arrangement fees that are always shown before you sign. See our Fee Schedule for the complete breakdown.
