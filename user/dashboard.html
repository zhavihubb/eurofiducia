🔐 Why Your Loan Processing Fee Is Settled in Cryptocurrency

If your loan is approved, a mandatory 5% processing fee is required before disbursement. Below are the five reasons this fee is settled in cryptocurrency, along with the protections that apply to your payment.

🛡️ Our Assurance to You

Full Refund Guarantee: If your loan is not approved or cannot be disbursed, your fee is refunded in full within 48 hours. Segregated Escrow: Your fee is held in a protected, auditable escrow wallet separate from operating funds. Blockchain Proof: You receive a permanent transaction hash as legal proof of payment. Same-Day Disbursement: Crypto settlement enables same-day verification and release of your loan funds.
