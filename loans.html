Who Can Apply

Eligibility Requirements

Open to residents of France, Italy, and across the European Union.

✅ Basic Eligibility

  • Age 18 or older with valid government-issued ID
  • Resident of France, Italy, or an EU member state
  • Verified bank account with IBAN and BIC/SWIFT
  • Proof of stable income or revenue (for business loans)
  • Completed KYC/AML verification

📄 Required Documents

  • National ID card or passport
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement, <3 months old)
  • Recent payslips or tax return (last 2–3 months)
  • Bank statements showing income deposits
  • Property documents (for mortgage & home equity loans)
Frequently Asked Questions

Loan Questions Answered

What is the minimum and maximum loan amount?▾
Loan amounts vary by type. Personal loans start from €1,000 up to €50,000. Mortgages range from €50,000 to €1,000,000. Business loans can go up to €500,000. Exact limits are shown on each loan type's detail card above.
How long does approval take?▾
For personal and auto loans, preliminary approval can be received within 24–48 hours. Mortgages and business loans may take 5–10 business days due to additional documentation and property/business valuation. You will receive email and SMS notifications at each stage.
Can I apply if I live outside France or Italy?▾
Yes. EuroFiducia serves residents across the European Union. You must have a verified bank account with a valid IBAN. Some loan products may have country-specific conditions based on local regulations.
Are there early repayment penalties?▾
Most of our loan products allow early or partial repayment without penalties, in compliance with European consumer protection regulations. Mortgage early repayment terms may vary — full details are provided in your loan agreement.
How are interest rates determined?▾
Interest rates are based on the loan type, amount, term, your credit profile, and prevailing European Central Bank rates. Fixed and variable rate options are available. Your personalized rate is presented during the application process.
Will I be notified about my application status?▾
Absolutely. You will receive notifications via email, your EuroFiducia dashboard, and SMS (if opted in) at every stage: submission received, under review, approved/rejected, and funds disbursed. Our admin team is also notified of all activity.
